FLASH (0x08000000 - 0x0801FFFF, 128KB)
├── 0x08000000-0x0800003F: Vector Table (64 bytes)
│   ├── 0x08000000: Initial MSP (0x20002000)
│   ├── 0x08000004: Reset Handler (0x080001D0)
│   ├── 0x08000008-0x08000034: Missing handlers (0x00000000)
│   ├── 0x08000038: PendSV Handler (0x08000320)
│   └── 0x0800003C: SysTick Handler (0x08000290)
├── 0x08000040-0x08000430: .text section (1072 bytes)
│   ├── reset_handler: 0x080001D0
│   ├── task1_code: 0x08000290
│   ├── task2_code: 0x08000298
│   ├── systick_handler: 0x080002A0
│   ├── fire_PendSV: 0x080002E8
│   ├── pendsv_handler: 0x08000320
│   ├── mutex_lock: 0x080003B0
│   └── mutex_unlock: 0x080003E0
└── 0x08000430-0x0801FFFF: Unused

RAM (0x20000000 - 0x20001FFF, 8KB)
├── 0x20000000-0x20000400: task1_stack (1024 bytes)
│   └── _task1_stack_top: 0x20000400
├── 0x20000400-0x20000800: task2_stack (1024 bytes)
│   └── _task2_stack_top: 0x20000800
├── 0x20000800-0x20000804: tick_count (4 bytes)
├── 0x20000810-0x20000830: tcb_task1 (32 bytes)
│   ├── [0]  PSP
│   ├── [4]  NEXT
│   ├── [8]  PREV
│   ├── [12] STATE
│   ├── [16] WAKE_TICK
│   ├── [20] PRIORITY
│   ├── [24] MUTEX_WAIT
│   └── [28] MUTEX_NEXT
├── 0x20000830-0x20000850: tcb_task2 (32 bytes)
│   └── (Same structure as tcb_task1)
├── 0x20000850-0x20000854: current_tcb (4 bytes)
├── 0x20000854-0x20000858: tcb_linked_list_head (4 bytes)
├── 0x20000860-0x20000868: UART_MUTEX (8 bytes)
│   ├── [0]  OWNER (TCB Address or 0)
│   └── [4]  WAIT_LIST (TCB Address or 0)
├── 0x20000868-0x20000870: ANY_MUTEX (8 bytes)
│   └── (Same structure as UART_MUTEX)
└── 0x20000870-0x20001FFF: Unused
